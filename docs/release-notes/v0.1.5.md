# rp-macro-writer 0.1.5 – Release Notes

Cleaner target conditions for cast sequences and the version in the window title.

## What's new
- **Cast sequences always end with `[@target]`:** the last bracket of every `/castsequence` line is `[@target]`, so the spell goes to your current target when no earlier bracket matches. Without any condition the line starts with just `[@target]`.
- **Target of the target:** every bracket of the chain also checks `exists`, matching a line that is known to work in game: `[harm,nodead,exists][@targettarget,harm,nodead,exists][@target]`.
- **No more duplicated conditions for focus and mouseover:** `[@focus,harm,nodead]` instead of `[@focus,harm,nodead][harm,nodead]`. If you want the old fallback to your current target, tick the new option "Rückfall auf aktuelles Ziel" (fall back to current target).
- **Version in the title:** the window title shows the installed version.

## Good to know
- Macros already in game stay as they are until you save them again.
- A macro still holds at most 255 characters. The `[@target]` bracket adds 9 characters to every cast sequence; longer macros are shortened automatically where possible.
- Not yet fully tested in game on WoW Forever or Retail.

## Supported game flavors
WoW Forever, Retail 12.1.x, Mists Classic, Wrath (Titan Reforged), TBC Classic, Classic Era.
