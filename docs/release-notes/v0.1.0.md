# MakroWriter 0.1.0 – Release Notes (draft)

> Status: draft. Test in game, add the release date and screenshots before publishing.

**MakroWriter builds macros by drag and drop.** Drag spells from your spellbook into the window, tick a few boxes on the right, done – without typing a single line of macro text.

## What the first version can do
- **Drag instead of type:** spells, pet abilities and items go into the step list by drag and drop.
- **Detects what you need:** one spell becomes a simple cast, several become a cast sequence – including a reset on target change, on leaving combat or after a set time.
- **Common conditions with one click:** enemies only, living (or dead) targets only, mouseover or focus first.
- **More than sequences:** bind spells to Shift / Ctrl / Alt, cast a random spell or generate several lines.
- **Made with warlocks in mind:** combine DoTs and curses freely, send your pet in at the same time, and avoid clipping your channels.
- **Preview and character counter:** the finished macro text is always visible, with the 255-character limit in view.
- **Save and reuse:** combinations are kept and can be loaded and adjusted later.

## How to use it
1. Type `/mw`.
2. Open your spellbook and drag spells into the window.
3. Enter a macro name and click the create button.
4. Use the pick-up button to place the macro on an action bar.

## Good to know
- WoW allows only one spell on the global cooldown per key press. A cast sequence therefore casts the next spell in the list with each press.
- Macros cannot be created or changed while in combat.
- A macro holds at most 255 characters (umlauts count twice).
- The addon's interface is currently German only.

## Supported game flavors
Retail 12.1.x, Mists Classic, Wrath (Titan Reforged), TBC Classic, Forever, Classic Era.
