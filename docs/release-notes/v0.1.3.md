# rp-macro-writer 0.1.3 – Release Notes

A one-click rotation, a minimap button and automatic shortening of long macros.

## What's new
- **Best rotation:** the "Beste Rotation" button fills the step list with a rotation for your level and the spells you currently know, and creates the macro.
- **Minimap button:** click it to open or close the window, drag it around the minimap edge. Its position is remembered.
- **Macro icon:** the icon of the first step is shown at the top right of the options, next to the macro type.
- **Automatic shortening:** a macro that would exceed 255 characters is shortened without changing what it does (no spaces after commas and semicolons in spell lists, `/tar` instead of `/target`).

## Good to know
- **Not every class has a best rotation available.** The addon asks the game's own rotation helper first. Where the game offers none for your class, specialization or game flavor, only warlocks get the addon's built-in fallback; for all other classes the button reports that no rotation is available.
- A macro still holds at most 255 characters. If shortening is not enough, remove spells or options.
- Not yet fully tested in game on WoW Forever or Retail.

## Supported game flavors
WoW Forever, Retail 12.1.x, Mists Classic, Wrath (Titan Reforged), TBC Classic, Classic Era.
