# rp-macro-writer 0.1.4 – Release Notes

An English interface, reordering steps by dragging, and a best rotation for every class.

## What's new
- **English interface:** all window texts, tooltips, chat messages and warnings are available in English. German game clients keep the German texts; every other client language gets English.
- **Reorder steps by dragging:** press a step in the list, drag it onto another step and release. The arrow buttons still work.
- **Best rotation for every class and race:** the "Best rotation" button asks the game's own rotation helper first. Where the game offers nothing, a simple built-in table covering all 13 classes takes over. Your race does not matter, because the spells come from your own spellbook, and spells that your game version does not have are skipped.

## Good to know
- The built-in rotation table is a rough heuristic, not a simulated optimum, and its spell IDs are not yet verified in game.
- A macro still holds at most 255 characters. Longer macros are shortened automatically where possible.
- Not yet fully tested in game on WoW Forever or Retail.

## Supported game flavors
WoW Forever, Retail 12.1.x, Mists Classic, Wrath (Titan Reforged), TBC Classic, Classic Era.
